- Accompany vs. accompaniment: Accompany is a verb; accompaniment is a noun.

- Actual vs. actually: Actual describes what is real; actually modifies a statement to indicate what is really true.
- Additional vs. additive: Additional means extra; additive usually refers to something added to another substance or product.
- Address: You can address a person or audience by speaking to them, or address a problem by dealing with it directly.
