- Accompany vs. accompaniment: Accompany is a verb; accompaniment is a noun.
- Accomplish vs. accomplishment: Accomplish means achieve; accomplishment is the achievement itself.
- Account vs. accountant vs. accounting: a record or explanation; the professional who handles financial records; the discipline or system of handling those records.

- Acquire vs. acquisition: Acquire is a verb; acquisition is the process or result of obtaining something.
- Actual vs. actually: Actual describes what is real; actually modifies a statement to indicate what is really true.
- Additional vs. additive: Additional means extra; additive usually refers to something added to another substance or product.
- Address: You can address a person or audience by speaking to them, or address a problem by dealing with it directly.

15\. Alignment — an arrangement in a straight line or appropriate position; agreement or cooperation between people, goals, or organizations.

- The technician checked the alignment of the printer's moving parts.
- The company's new strategy requires alignment between its departments and long-term objectives.

16\. Alike — similar to each other; in the same way or to the same degree.

- The two brothers look remarkably alike despite having different personalities.
- The new policy affects experienced employees and beginners alike.

17\. Allergic — having an immune-system reaction to a particular substance; extremely sensitive or opposed to something in figurative use.

- She is allergic to peanuts and must carefully check food labels.
- He seems allergic to criticism and becomes defensive whenever someone offers feedback.

18\. Allergy — an immune-system reaction to a substance that is usually harmless to most people.

- His allergy to pollen causes sneezing during the spring.
- The restaurant asks customers to report any food allergy before ordering.

19\. Alliance — a partnership or union formed for mutual benefit, protection, or a shared objective.

- The two countries formed an alliance to improve regional security.
- The technology companies established an alliance to develop a shared software platform.

20\. Allocate — to distribute or assign resources, money, time, or responsibilities according to a plan.

- The manager allocated additional funds to the software development team.
- You should allocate enough time for revision before your examination.

21\. Allot — to give or distribute a specific portion or share to someone.

- Each student was allotted twenty minutes to complete the presentation.
- The committee allotted a portion of its budget to employee training.

22\. Allowance — an amount of money or another resource given regularly or permitted for a particular purpose; permission or consideration granted in certain contexts.

- His parents give him a monthly allowance to cover personal expenses.
- The budget includes an allowance for unexpected maintenance costs.

23\. Alloy — a mixture of two or more elements, at least one of which is a metal; to mix substances to form such a material.

- Brass is an alloy made primarily of copper and zinc.
- Engineers use an alloy of metals to improve the strength of certain machine components.

24\. Ally — a person, group, or country that cooperates with another toward a common goal.

- The small business found an ally in a larger company that supported its expansion.
- During the conflict, the two countries worked closely with their allies.

25\. Alone — without other people; by oneself.

- She prefers to study alone because it helps her concentrate.
- Although he lived alone, he regularly met his friends and family.

26\. Along — moving forward or following the length of something; accompanying someone or progressing together.

- We walked along the river until we reached the old bridge.
- My colleague came along with me to the conference.

## Quick distinctions to remember

- Agreement vs. alliance: An agreement is a shared decision or arrangement; an alliance is a partnership formed to pursue common interests.
- Aid vs. allowance: Aid provides help or support; an allowance is a permitted or allocated amount of money or resources.
- Aim vs. air: Aim means direct toward a target or intend to achieve something; air can mean broadcast or express something publicly.
- Alarm vs. alert: An alarm signals danger or expresses fear; an alert is a warning or a state of watchfulness.
- Ailment vs. allergy: An ailment is an illness or health problem; an allergy is a specific immune reaction to a substance.
- Allocate vs. allot: Both mean distribute, but allocate often refers to assigning resources according to a plan, while allot emphasizes giving someone a specific share.
- Alike vs. alone: Alike means similar; alone means by oneself.
- Ally vs. alliance: An ally is a partner; an alliance is the partnership itself.
- Alien vs. alienate: Alien describes something foreign or unfamiliar; alienate means to make someone feel isolated or to transfer ownership.
- Along vs. alike: Along often describes direction or movement; alike describes similarity.
